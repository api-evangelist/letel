# Letel (Shanghai Letel Communication Equipment (Group) Co., Ltd.)

> Chinese manufacturer of information and communication network infrastructure,
> founded March 1992 and headquartered in Shanghai. Letel builds data center
> physical infrastructure — cabinet, cold-aisle and containerized micromodules,
> IT cabins, and cabinets — alongside communication cabling, optical
> communication devices, data communication equipment, broadband access
> equipment, and structural components for notebooks and servers.

## Status

Letel publishes no public API, no developer portal, and no developer
documentation. This entry exists in the API Evangelist network as a company
profile, not an API profile. It was surfaced as a portfolio company of Qiming
Venture Partners.
